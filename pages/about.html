About PolyLog

The story behind your all-in-one gaming hub.

What is PolyLog?

PolyLog is a free, open gaming hub that aggregates game data, news, deals, and upcoming releases into one polished interface. No accounts required — just explore.

Data Sources

All data is fetched live from trusted public APIs:

  • 🎮 RAWG — Game database (500K+ titles)
  • 💰 CheapShark — PC game price comparisons
  • 📰 IGN RSS — Gaming news and reviews
  • 📰 GameSpot RSS — Gaming news and reviews
  • 🎬 YouTube — Trailer search links
