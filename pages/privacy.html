Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025. PolyLog respects your privacy.

Data We Collect

PolyLog uses Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymous usage statistics (page views, session duration). No personally identifiable information is collected. Your wishlist is stored entirely in your browser's localStorage and never sent to any server.

Third-Party APIs

Game data is fetched from RAWG.io, CheapShark, IGN, and GameSpot. Requests to these services are subject to their respective privacy policies. PolyLog does not store or forward any personal data to these services.

Cookies

Google Analytics may set cookies for analytics purposes. You can opt out via your browser settings or the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
